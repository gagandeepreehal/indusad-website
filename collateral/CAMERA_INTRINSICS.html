Resolution and geometric use

The calibration declares 1920 x 1080. The inspected recording has 1280 x 720 camera media. For the current devkit workflow, the publisher confirmed pure 1920×1080-to-1280×720 resizing without cropping on 23 October 2026. The adapter requires the explicit intrinsic_image_transform: "resize" policy and scales focal lengths, principal point and skew while retaining dimensionless distortion coefficients. See published sensor acceptance. This is producer-supplied pipeline information, not independent per-camera accuracy validation; the coefficient order/model limitation above remains. The reported RMSE has no verified unit or evaluation procedure in this reference.

These values alone do not establish camera-to-ego, IMU-to-camera, or camera-to-LiDAR extrinsics.
