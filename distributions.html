Braking and sudden stops

INDUS-AD has 1,670 braking-to-stop candidates across 805 recordings, including 101 sudden stops. The screen used 69.03 hours of speed intervals from 1,441 of 1,469 recordings.

The 5,447 stopped windows describe time spent near stationary. Each braking-to-stop event counts one observed transition to near standstill. An event requires a speed of at least 7.2 km/h in the previous five seconds and at least one second near standstill. The approach speed has no upper limit.
